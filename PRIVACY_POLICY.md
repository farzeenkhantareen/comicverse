# Privacy Policy

ComicVerse is designed to be fully compliant with Google Play Console guidelines.

## Key Policies
1. **Offline-First**: All data, reading history, search parameters, and settings are cached locally.
2. **Zero Tracking**: We do not include any tracking tools, user analytics packages, or third-party ads.
3. **No Permissions**: No unnecessary permissions (such as location, files/media access, or background activities) are requested.
